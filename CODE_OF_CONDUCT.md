# OpenRec Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in OpenRec a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Focus criticism on ideas and observable behavior, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing credentials or personal information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn when mistakes happen.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and
sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces or when representing OpenRec publicly.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the organization maintainers through the contact method listed
on the OpenRec organization profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue warnings,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants. Reports will be handled with
appropriate confidentiality and conflicts of interest will be avoided where possible.

This policy is based on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
